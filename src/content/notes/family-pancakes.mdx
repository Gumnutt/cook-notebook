I love to make these for my wife and kids on Saturday mornings. They are fluffy and dead simple to make. Don't skimp on the the good stuff! Maple syrup, lemon and sugar or fresh berries really make this breakfast something else.

## Ingredients
- 2 cups Self Raising Flour
- 1 tbsp Sugar
- 1 3/4 cups Milk
- 1 Egg
- 3 tbsp Butter, melted

## Method
1. Mix together the Flour and Sugar.
2. Add the milk, Egg and melted Butter.
3. Mix until fully combined. Do not overmix the batter as you will have tough pancakes.
4. Preheat a pan and melt 1 tbsp of butter.
5. Cook for 2-3 minutes on the first side. When you see air bubbles coming up through the pancake and the edges start to look dry, flip those suckers.

## Notes

For fluffier pancakes, let the batter rest for 10-15 minutes before cooking. You can also add a pinch of salt to the dry ingredients to enhance the flavor.
